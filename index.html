Estimating & Operations Agent

Processes request-for-quotes (RFQs), drafts base estimations from material sheets, coordinates vendor notifications, and tracks project permit documents.

  • Quote drafting & itemized cost calculators
  • Subcontractor and supplier RFP messaging
  • Permit tracker & submission status updates
  • Document scanning & invoice processing

# Extracting materials and matching price libraries...

print(estimate.summary)
